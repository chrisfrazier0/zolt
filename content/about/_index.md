This is the demo site for **zolt**, an opinionated skeleton for
[Zola](https://www.getzola.org/). It shows the pieces zolt sets up for you —
long-form **articles** and photo **galleries**, woven together on the home page
by date — on top of a deliberately minimal baseline you're meant to replace.
