The morning started at the water. The harbour was quiet — just the sound of
rope against mast and gulls picking over the tide line. I took a few frames
before anyone else was about.

{{ <gallery galleries={page.extra.galleries} name="harbor" base_path={page.colocated_path} /> }}

By mid-morning the town had woken up, and the square filled with the weekly
market. This is the part I always look forward to: a dozen stalls crammed into
one narrow street, each one louder and more colourful than the last. I could
have spent the whole day here.
